# Vision

**Banyan.ai — The Product Distribution OS for founders.**
*You build it. Banyan gets it out.*

> Customer-facing positioning and MVP scope live in [`STRATEGY.md`](STRATEGY.md).
> This document remains the internal product vision and engineering philosophy.

## The problem

Shipping a product is now faster than finding the people who need it. Distribution
is improvised: a post here, a thread there, decided the same day, with no audience,
no objective and no way to tell what worked. Teams produce content volume and call
it progress.

## What Banyan.ai is

A system that turns distribution into an operating loop a single founder can run:

1. **Understand** — capture the product, audience, goal and brand voice.
2. **Plan** — draft a hypothesis-framed distribution plan, reviewable and editable.
3. **Campaign** — one message, one audience, one declared success metric.
4. **Create** — channel- and format-appropriate drafts in your voice, versioned.
5. **Approve** — a human approves a specific revision; nothing publishes without it.
6. **Publish** — through a real integration, or recorded manually with a live URL.
7. **Measure** — first-party tracked links, clicks, attributed signups.
8. **Learn** — experiments draft conclusions from recorded data, or say "not enough data".

## Who it is for

Solo founders and small product teams taking a first product to market — people who
own both the product and the growth problem, and who cannot afford a growth team.

## The banyan metaphor

A banyan tree grows outwards from a single trunk and puts down new roots where its
branches touch the ground. Distribution works the same way: one clear position,
many channels, and each result becoming new ground to grow from.

- **Roots** — product context, audience research, positioning.
- **Trunk** — the shared plan, campaigns and approval control.
- **Branches** — the channels you actually run.
- **Canopy** — reach and attention.
- **Growth rings** — experiments and the learning they leave behind.

The metaphor informs the model, not the interface.

## Principles

1. **Agentic by design** — the system does the coordinating work, not just the writing.
2. **Reliability before autonomy** — a workflow that behaves correctly beats a demo that impresses.
3. **Human control over consequential actions** — publishing is approved, never assumed.
4. **Evidence over fabricated intelligence** — no invented metrics, testimonials or research.
5. **Measurable outcomes over vanity metrics** — outcomes, not output.
6. **Honest empty states** — a real zero and "not measured yet" are different things and are shown differently.
7. **Real integrations over mock success** — a provider that is not configured says so.
8. **Progressive autonomy** — earn trust step by step; autonomy is enabled, scoped and auditable.
9. **Low operating cost** — one app, one worker, one database; no infrastructure theatre.
10. **Banyan.ai distributes Banyan.ai first** — it is its own first customer.

## What success looks like

Tracked as three separate, honest milestones — never as one vague "done":

1. **Capability** — the product can run the whole loop.
2. **Reliability** — the loop behaves correctly under real conditions.
3. **Growth** — real campaigns produce measurable business outcomes.

Milestone 3 is only real when attributed signups are actually observed. See
[`progress.md`](progress.md) for where each stands today.

## Explicit non-goals

- A generic AI writing assistant.
- A social scheduler with an LLM bolted on.
- A dashboard of invented analytics.
- A multi-agent framework whose parts exist for appearance rather than work.
